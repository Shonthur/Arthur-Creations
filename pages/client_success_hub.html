Our product

CampusFlow

CampusFlow is a smart student platform built by Avalon Corporate Solutions. It puts timetables, announcements, notes, a campus map, a marketplace and printing requests in one place for students.

Visit CampusFlow
What the site includes
  • Lecture and exam timetable and an academic calendar
  • Course announcements, notes and self study, and an AI academic tutor
  • Campus map and finder, a campus marketplace and a printing and admin desk
  • Friends and connections, a games lounge, light and dark themes

Shon Sneakers Ug

A sneaker shop with a product catalogue, prices in UGX, an add-to-cart button and a cart counter. The owner can add new products from the page.

What the site includes
  • Product cards with photo, name, short description and price
  • Add to cart with a live cart counter
  • Owner button to add a new product
