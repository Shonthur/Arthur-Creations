FAQ

Questions people ask

Quick answers about prices, time and support.

How much does a website cost?

Packages start at UGX 150,000 (Express). Pro starts from UGX 300,000 and Premium is a custom quote. You can work out an estimate with the price calculator. The final price is confirmed in writing before any work starts.

How long will my website take?

It depends on the size and the features. You get a delivery time in your quote. If you need it sooner, the calculator has faster delivery options.

What do I need to give you?

Your business name, contact details and a short description of what you do. A logo, photos and text help a lot. If you do not have them yet, tell me and we will decide together how to get them.

Will my website work on phones?

Yes. Every website is built mobile friendly, and I design for small screens first.

What happens after the website is live?

Express includes 30 days of support after launch and Pro includes 90 days. Premium includes ongoing maintenance.

Can I update the website myself?

The Pro package includes content management and training, so you can learn how to change your own content.

Do you only build websites?

No. I also do graphic design (flyers, brochures, calendars, business cards, receipts), short videos and motion pieces, and IT and network support.

What about a domain name and hosting?

A domain name (like yourbusiness.com) and hosting are paid to the company that provides them. I can explain the options in simple words before you choose.

How do I start?

Use the Start a project form, or message me on WhatsApp. Tell me what you need and I will reply with a clear plan and price.

Ready to start?

Send me a message with what you need. I will reply with a clear plan.
